Install on Phone (Standalone App)

Instant Access from your Phone

This application is 100% self-contained in this single file. You can install it straight to your home screen so it runs full-screen with offline caching.

1 Host for Free (Takes 30 seconds):

Drag and drop this file to app.netlify.com/drop or upload it to GitHub Pages. You'll get an instant web link (e.g. https://your-name.netlify.app).

2 Add to Home Screen:
  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button → tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu → tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install".
3 Offline & Permanent:

All your daily ticks, boxing rounds, and trade hours are safely saved in your phone's storage. Remember to tap "Backup JSON" occasionally to export your progress.
